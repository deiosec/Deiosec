PROJECT — 02

آرامش

Concept: A minimal poster focused on calmness, simplicity and taking a quiet moment for yourself.

Tool: Canva

Type: Poster Design

The Idea

The poster was created around the idea of آرامش — creating a simple visual atmosphere with enough empty space to communicate a feeling of calmness.

The Process

The composition was built in Canva using simple visual elements, careful typography and a limited color palette. The placement of the elements was kept minimal to preserve the calm feeling of the design.
