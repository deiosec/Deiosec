PROJECT — 06

PLANT POT

Concept: A plant pot constructed from simple geometric shapes with a focus on proportion, balance and depth.

Tool: Canva

Type: Geometric Design

The Idea

The goal was to turn basic geometric shapes into a simple and recognizable plant pot while keeping the composition clean.

The Process

The pot and plant were constructed in Canva using simple shapes. Different layers were combined to build the object and create a sense of depth while keeping the overall design minimal.
